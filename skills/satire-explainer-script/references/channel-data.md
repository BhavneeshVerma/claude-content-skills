## What the numbers say

1. **One topic, eleven times.** Every video looks at money and excess through the eyes of a broke narrator. The topic is not decoration. It is the engine: it combines aspiration and schadenfreude, and it is universally relatable.
2. **Question titles now beat tier lists.** The last four tier-style videos average about 3.8M views. The last five question or "you" videos average about 11M. Copy the current format, not the 2023 one.
3. **Short does not lose.** The two biggest videos run 9 minutes. Length follows the material, not a target.
4. **The biggest videos are the most educational.** "Most Expensive Thing" (20.7M) and "$1 Trillion" (12.4M) explain real things (land value, megaproject ROI, antimatter, medical-debt economics), with lighter, less edgy jokes than the tier lists. The channel is drifting from satire toward infotainment, and the numbers reward it.
5. **He already dubs into Hindi.** The newest video has about 20 AI-dubbed audio tracks, including Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam and Punjabi. A Hindi channel in this style competes with him directly. Win on local stories, not on translated ones.
6. **Pacing (all 11 transcripts):** 167 to 207 words per minute, which is fast. A 3-second-plus gap in the narration (a picture-only beat) every 8 to 12 seconds, about 4 to 8 per minute.
